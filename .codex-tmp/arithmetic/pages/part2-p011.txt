How to proceed?
1.   Low and High are updated at each step as follows:
     NewHigh:=OldLow+Range*HighRange(X);
     NewLow:=OldLow+Range*LowRange(X);
     where
     Range=OldHigh−OldLow and
     LowRange(X) and HighRange(X) are the lower bound and the upper
        bound of the interval corresponding to X, respectively.
2.   At each step the length of the current range is
     NewRange= NewHigh - NewLow=
                =Range*(HighRange(X) – LowRange(X))

 Arithmetic coding vs. Huffman coding

A detailed comparison is provided in
Bookstein, A., & Klein, S. T. (1993). Is Huffman coding dead?
Computing, 50(4), 279–296.
In a typical English-language text, space is the most common symbol,
with a probability of about 18%, so Huffman's upper limit of
redundancy is quite small.
On the contrary, in black and white images, arithmetic encoding is
much better than Huffman's unless a block (group) technique is used
Huffman decoding is generally faster

bit stream.

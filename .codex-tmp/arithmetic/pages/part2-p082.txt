     Mid-term exam
The midterm exam will take place on April 16th (new date), at 9:00 AM in Room 5 of
the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science. Registration through the
student portal is required in order to take the exam. The exam will last 75 minutes
will consist of five exercises.
❑The exercises may be either theoretical or problem-solving. The topics of the
exercises will cover lectures from 1 to 7.
❑Examples include:
  ❑What is the average code length and its relation to entropy?
  ❑Given a certain code, determine whether it is uniquely decodable (UD), explaining the
   method used.
❑The final exam will include five questions, as well. The final grade will be given by
the average of the grade from the two written tests (midterm and final).
❑Those who take the midterm can choose to have their final grade recorded, if it is at
least 18, or try to improve by the oral exam.
❑For the others, the written exam and the oral exam are both mandatory.
